Paraíso Redesign

Paraíso, reimagined: fixing choke points, adding routes

  • Game/Engine: Overwatch
  • Role: Level Designer
  • Timeframe: December 2025 — Feburary 2026

Design Intent

To simply put, Paraíso is just too hard to play. It strays from the convential hybrid map layout where it has two chokepoints instead of just one, forcing players to spend additional time and resources before fighting around the capture point. Overwatch Twitch VODs indicate that the map shows up equally as much as the other hybrid maps but does not get voted on nearly as much in high rank lobbies (link below). This data isn't entirely representative of the entire player population within Overwatch, but it clearly shows that there is a problem with the map. This redesign aims to introduce new rotational opportunities while maintaining the current flow of the map.

Outcome & Reflection

Overall, the process of redesigning Paraíso was an experience that showcased the importance of planning, especially within popular live-service games like Overwatch where map changes are made only a few times each year. Identifying a clear and achievable objective is a vital step in any project planning process, which is part of the reason why I backtracked so much within the early stages of the project.

During the initial stages of the project, the redesign focused solely on the first section of the map, which was fine at first, but later complicated the flow of the entire map. Further thought had to be put into the idea behind the entire redesign. The design intent reflects the end result of this process. However, I'd like to add that introducing new rotational opportunities may provide players more freedom in their hero choice, hopefully making the map more attractive during the voting screen.

While I did learn a lot in this project from a multiplayer level design perspective, there are still things like documenting one's thought process, planning more thoroughly before the project, and conducting more user research that could've made the end result much better. I learned a lot about myself and where I need to improve in this project. For that, I am grateful.

An official redesign of the map Paraíso was announced on August 2nd, 2026 by Blizzard Entertainment
