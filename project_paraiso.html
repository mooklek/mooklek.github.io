Paraíso Redesign

Paraíso, reimagined: fixing choke points, adding routes

  • Game/Engine: Overwatch
  • Role: Level Designer
  • Timeframe: December 2025 — Feburary 2026

Design Intent

To simply put, Paraíso is just too hard to play. It strays from the convential hybrid map layout where it has two chokepoints instead of just one, forcing players to spend additional time and resources before fighting around the capture point. Overwatch Twitch VODs indicate that the map shows up equally as much as the other hybrid maps but does not get voted on nearly as much in high rank lobbies (link below). This data isn't entirely representative of the entire player population within Overwatch, but it clearly shows that there is a problem with the map. This redesign aims to introduce new rotational opportunities while maintaining the current flow of the map.
